FAQ

Common Questions

How do I update Platoon?

Open the App Store or Google Play, search for Platoon, and tap Update if one is available.

How do I post a trade?

Open Trades in the app, choose the shift you want to trade, add the details, and post it for members to see.

Why am I not getting notifications?

Check that notifications are turned on for Platoon in your phone settings and inside the app.

How do I update my schedule or profile?

Open your profile or schedule settings in Platoon. If something still looks wrong, contact support with your department and assignment details.

How do I start a chat?

Open Chats, find the member or conversation you need, and send your message from there.

Where do I find union announcements?

Open the union area in Platoon to see announcements, meetings, votes, documents, and resources.

Who should I contact if something looks wrong?

Send a support request with your name, department, and what you were trying to do.
